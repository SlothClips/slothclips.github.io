Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

SlothClips is a free desktop app for Windows. It has no user accounts. The makers of SlothClips never receive your videos, your API keys or your shorts.

What stays on your computer

  • Your videos, shorts, subtitles, gaming loops and watermarks - in the folders you choose.
  • Your settings, queues and notes - in %APPDATA%\SlothClips.
  • Your Gemini API keys - only if you tick "Remember", and then encrypted so only your Windows account can read them.

What is sent to other services

  • Google Gemini (using your own key): parts of the videos you choose (at low resolution), their audio or transcript, your rules and notes, and the text for voice intros. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products, and humans may review it - don't send anything private or confidential.
  • The websites you download from (for example YouTube) see the download requests, as with any download.
  • The app's free tools (FFmpeg, the YouTube downloader, speech models from Hugging Face) are downloaded from those projects' servers.

Ads

SlothClips is free because it shows ads. While your shorts are being made, the app shows a small ad panel loaded from this website. The ads are provided by a third-party ad network. Like any website ad, the ad network can see your IP address (which tells it roughly which country you're in), your device and browser type, and can use cookies or similar technology to choose ads, limit how often you see them and count views and clicks. The ad network's own privacy policy applies to that. The ads never see your videos, your keys or your shorts.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't use analytics on this site.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't collect your videos, keys, shorts or personal details.

Your choices

You can delete your data at any time: delete your shorts in the app or in their folder, remove your keys in Settings, or uninstall the app (the uninstaller can also remove your settings and shorts). You can clear ad cookies by clearing the app's browsing data or reinstalling it.
