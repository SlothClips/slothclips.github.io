FAQ

Questions

Is SlothClips really free?

Yes. No subscription, no credits, no paid plan, no trial that runs out. Make as many shorts as you want. SlothClips shows a few ads while your shorts are being made - that's how it stays free.

Is there really no limit?

SlothClips itself has no limit. It uses Google's Gemini AI with your own free key, and Google gives every key a generous free daily allowance. If you ever hit it, wait until the next day or add a second free key - the app switches between keys for you.

Do my shorts get a watermark?

Never. SlothClips doesn't add its name or logo to your videos. If you want your own logo or channel name on them, you can add it yourself.

Does it work for Instagram Reels and TikTok?

Yes. Every short is a 1080 × 1920 vertical video, the format used by YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No graphics card is needed. Any Windows 10 or 11 PC with 8 GB of RAM works well; it runs on 4 GB too, just slower. The installer picks the subtitle model that suits your PC.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

Not to us. Everything is made and saved on your computer. To find the best moments, the app sends parts of the video to Google's Gemini AI using your own key.

Can I clip any YouTube video?

Only use videos you own or have permission to use - for example your own channel, or creators' clipping campaigns that pay you to clip their content. You're responsible for what you post.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet - SlothClips is Windows only for now.
